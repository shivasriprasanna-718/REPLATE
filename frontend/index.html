How it works

From leftover to delivered, in four steps

Request pickup

A donor opens Replate and taps "Request Pickup," adding food type, quantity and photos.

Volunteer notified

The nearest available volunteer or partner org gets the request and heads to the location.

Checked & verified

Food is checked for safety and hygiene, packed, and given a "Verified Safe" badge.

Delivered with care

Food reaches an NGO or daycare center; the donor gets a notification and a Kindness Points badge.

Zero-waste, always

Food unsafe for people? It still doesn't go to a landfill.

Anything that fails our safety check is diverted to composting or cocopeat production instead — closing the loop and cutting greenhouse gas emissions from food waste.
